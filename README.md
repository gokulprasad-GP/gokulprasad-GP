### 🧭 My vision
Marketing should be the **growth engine of the business**, not a cost centre. I believe the next generation of marketing leadership blends **brand building, data, technology and creativity** into one system — where every investment is tied to customer value and long-term enterprise growth.

My journey has taken me from hands-on digital execution to leading strategy, teams and partners across multiple markets and industries. Along the way I've developed a **T-shaped leadership style**: broad, business-wide perspective with deep expertise in media, measurement and growth.

### 💡 What I believe
- **Customer first, always** — growth starts with understanding people, not channels
- **Brand and performance are one** — long-term brand equity and short-term results should reinforce each other
- **Data informs, stories inspire** — I use storytelling to turn complex data into decisions leaders can act on
- **Build capability, not dependency** — strong in-house teams, clear governance and true agency partnership
- **Stay curious** — AI and emerging technology are reshaping marketing; leaders must learn faster than the market
