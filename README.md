### 🚀 About me
Media leader with **10+ years** planning, buying and measuring media across **linear TV, radio, OOH, CTV, programmatic, search and social**. I currently lead media strategy for a major **automotive** manufacturer's four-brand portfolio ($100M+ annual investment) and previously managed $100M+ in annual media in the **gaming & lottery** sector.

I plan against weekly reach targets, measure efficiency through high-value website behaviours (HVBs), and connect both to sales using **media mix modeling (MMM)**. I also build in-house performance capability, agency governance, playbooks and Agile operating models.

- 📈 Agency scorecard results lifted from **72% → 90%** with KPI scorecards, SLAs and quarterly reviews
